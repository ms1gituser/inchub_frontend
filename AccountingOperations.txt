2 | KYC Document
Checklist &
Compliance Status | Every accounting client has a KYC compliance checklist that must be completed before full service begins. The KYC status is a persistent colour-coded indicator visible on every screen that shows the client record — red if any item is expired or missing, amber if items are approaching expiry, green if fully compliant. KYC status is checked and refreshed automatically every 24 hours. KYC is never considered permanently complete — documents expire and must be renewed.

KYC CHECKLIST ITEMS: Trade License (valid copy), MOA or Partnership Agreement, Passport copies (all owners and signatories), Emirates ID copies (all owners and signatories — UAE residents), Proof of registered address (Ejari or equivalent), Bank account confirmation letter (IBAN), UBO declaration (all beneficial owners above 25%), AML/CFT client risk assessment form (completed by IncHub CO), Source of Funds declaration (for HIGH-risk clients), Source of Wealth declaration (for HIGH-risk clients and PEPs). | Stage 1 — project created. KYC checklist auto-generated from client record. | 1. Generate KYC checklist from client template (Admin-configurable in Document Checklist Manager)

3. CLIENT RECORD COLOUR INDICATOR — persists on every screen showing client:
   RED: one or more KYC items missing OR expired OR expiring within 30 days
   AMBER: all items present but one or more expiring within 60 days
   GREEN: all items present, verified, and valid for more than 60 days
4. Automated expiry alerts:

TRANSACTION THRESHOLD MONITORING (tracks over 3 consecutive months):
— Month 1 over agreed count: positive notification to client ('Your business is growing — transaction volume increased this month. We are monitoring to ensure your agreement stays aligned with your needs.')

— Month 3 consecutive: the 3-month threshold is now confirmed. Generate addendum notification to client and accounting manager. Schedule a formal fee and agreement review. Produce draft addendum to the service agreement. This is the point at which the fee revision call becomes mandatory.
— Resets to zero if any month falls back within the agreed count before 3 consecutive months are reached. | Month's books finalised, vendor register updated, all suspense cleared. | 1. Generate monthly P&L statement, balance sheet summary, transaction count, monthly turnover, fees received status

6 | VAT Tracking,
Voluntary Registration
Log & Quarterly Filing | For VAT-registered clients: quarterly VAT filing tracked and managed. For non-VAT clients: rolling annual turnover tracked against UAE registration thresholds with a voluntary registration log starting from USD 50,000 (AED 185,000) — well before the mandatory AED 375,000 threshold.

VOLUNTARY REGISTRATION LOG (New in v10 — for non-VAT registered clients only):
When a non-VAT client's cumulative annual turnover reaches AED 185,000 (approx. USD 50,000), the system creates a formal Voluntary Registration Log entry and begins a structured notification sequence. If the client or accounting team waives the recommendation at any stage, the waiver is logged. A final emergency alert is triggered before the mandatory threshold to ensure penalties are avoided. | Monthly report completed — VAT track activates based on VAT registration status set at onboarding. | VAT REGISTERED TRACK:
1. Track VAT quarter dates from project configuration
2. Alert accounting officer at Day -21 before quarter end to begin VAT return preparation
3. Prepare VAT return using monthly transaction data — client must approve before filing
4. Send draft VAT return to client via portal + email (include FTA due date prominently)
5. File VAT return after client approval (MANDATORY — never file without client sign-off)
6. Mark VAT quarter FILED with date. Update client portal VAT dashboard.
7. Reminders: 90 / 60 / 30 days and 14 days before each quarter-end deadline

VAT FILING REMINDERS (All configurable by Admin):
— Day -90: 'VAT quarter ending in 90 days. Begin gathering records.'
— Day -60: 'VAT return preparation should begin now.'
— Day -28: 'VAT return due in 28 days. Draft return must be with client for approval.'
— Day -14: 'URGENT — VAT return due in 14 days. Client approval needed immediately.'
— Day -5: 'CRITICAL — VAT return due in 5 days. File immediately after client approval.'

NON-VAT CLIENT — VOLUNTARY REGISTRATION LOG:
1. At AED 185,000 cumulative turnover: CREATE VOLUNTARY REGISTRATION LOG ENTRY. Send recommendation to client and accounting officer: 'Your turnover has reached AED 185,000 (USD 50,000). UAE VAT voluntary registration is now available and recommended for input tax recovery benefits. IncHub can handle this registration.'
2. If client or officer acknowledges but defers: LOG the deferral with date and reason. Continue monitoring.
3. At AED 250,000: second recommendation + flag to accounting manager.
4. At AED 350,000: URGENT flag. Alert client, accounting officer, and manager. 'Mandatory registration threshold is approaching. IncHub strongly recommends immediate voluntary registration.'
5. At AED 375,000: EMERGENCY LOG ENTRY. Alert client + accounting officer + manager + CEO. 'Mandatory VAT registration threshold reached. Registration must be filed within 30 days. FTA penalties apply for non-compliance. IncHub will initiate registration immediately — please confirm to proceed.' This alert CANNOT be dismissed without a logged reason from manager or CEO.
6. If waiver at any stage: log who waived, when, and why. The emergency alert at AED 375,000 overrides any previous waiver and cannot be suppressed. | Relational database (VAT quarter tracking + rolling turnover + voluntary registration log)
