SURFACE 1
Internal Team Chat
(CRM-Embedded) | IncHub staff — sales reps, ops officers, accounting executives, managers, CEO | Answer process questions: 'What documents are needed for DMCC formation?' or 'What is the VAT threshold?' — Answer UAE compliance questions — Advise on next steps for a specific client or project — Participate in group chats when tagged or when AI determines its input is useful — Summarise meeting notes and generate action items post-meeting — Draft internal communications — Suggest responses to client queries | Full IncHub process documentation (all 17 blueprint sheets), UAE regulations (VAT law, CT law, AML/CFT law, Labour Law, free zone rules), all active project statuses and client records (accessed via CRM API — AI reads but never writes to live records), all historical client interactions and outcomes, all internal SOPs uploaded by Admin | If the question requires a human decision or involves a compliance judgement call: AI flags clearly — 'This requires human review. I can draft a response for your review.' Never gives definitive compliance rulings on its own authority. | All AI conversations logged to the relevant CRM record (client, lead, or project). Staff can mark any AI response as Helpful or Not Helpful — this feeds the training loop. | AI participates in group chats PASSIVELY (reads all messages) and speaks ONLY when: (a) directly tagged by a team member, (b) a question is asked that has a clear factual answer, or (c) Admin has enabled proactive mode (AI identifies relevant moments to contribute). Proactive mode toggle is Admin-configurable.
SURFACE 2
Client Portal Chat
(Existing Clients) | Logged-in existing clients via client portal | Answer questions about: their specific project status, their document checklist, their VAT/CT obligations and deadlines, their renewal dates, IncHub services and pricing, UAE business compliance requirements — Book a call or meeting via Calendly integration — Raise a support request that routes to the correct team member — Provide guidance on what documents to upload and why | For each logged-in client: their full CRM record, project status, task statuses, KYC status, VAT and CT filing history, renewal database, all past portal messages. Plus IncHub general knowledge base. Client never needs to re-introduce themselves or explain their company — the AI already knows. | If client asks something requiring human input (e.g. a fee negotiation, a complaint, a complex compliance question): AI responds helpfully with what it knows, then says 'Our team will follow up within [X hours]' and creates a CRM task for the relevant team member. | Every portal chat conversation logged to client record. AI captures: the question asked, the response given, whether the client was satisfied (thumbs up/down), and whether a human handoff was triggered. | AI learns from every client interaction — if a question is answered incorrectly and a human later provides the correct answer, the correction is fed back into the training data. All AI learning events are logged for audit purposes.
SURFACE 3
Website Chat
(New and Unknown Visitors) | New visitors on inchub.ae who are not yet in the CRM | Answer general questions about IncHub services, fees, UAE free zones, setup timelines — Qualify the visitor's intent (what are they looking to do, what is their timeline, which jurisdiction) — Capture initial details: full name, email address, WhatsApp number, company name (if applicable), purpose of contact, preferred meeting time — Book a consultation via Calendly — Create a new lead record in CRM with all captured details and route to the correct sales pipeline | IncHub general knowledge base: all services, fees, free zone comparisons, UAE business setup guides, FAQs. Does NOT have access to any existing client records. | If visitor asks a very specific legal or compliance question beyond the chatbot's scope: 'That is a great question and I want to make sure you get an accurate answer. Let me connect you with our team.' Triggers lead capture form. | Full lead capture: name, email, WhatsApp, company name, service interest, preferred consultation time. All data immediately creates a lead record in the Corp Sales Pipeline (or Accounting Sales Pipeline if service interest is accounting) and triggers the AI outreach sequence. | The website chatbot is the 24x7 lead generation tool. It never sleeps. Every conversation where contact details are captured creates a CRM lead automatically — no manual data entry by any team member.
